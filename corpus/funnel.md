# The marketing funnel: TOFU, MOFU, BOFU

The funnel describes the stages a prospect moves through on the way to becoming
a customer.

Top of the funnel (TOFU) is awareness. The audience is broad and barely knows
the brand. Content here is educational and low-commitment: blog posts, short
videos, social content. The goal is reach, not conversion.

Middle of the funnel (MOFU) is consideration. The prospect has a problem and is
comparing ways to solve it. Content gets more specific: comparisons, webinars,
case studies, gated guides that capture a lead in exchange for depth.

Bottom of the funnel (BOFU) is decision. The prospect is close to buying and
needs reassurance: demos, free trials, pricing pages, testimonials. Conversion
rate matters more than reach here.

Measuring each stage separately matters because a leak at TOFU and a leak at
BOFU need completely different fixes.
