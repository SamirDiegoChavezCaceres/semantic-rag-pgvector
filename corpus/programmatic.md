# Programmatic advertising basics

Programmatic advertising is the automated buying and selling of ad inventory
through real-time auctions, rather than manual insertion orders negotiated by
hand.

When a page loads, an ad exchange runs an auction for the available impression.
Demand-side platforms (DSPs) bid on behalf of advertisers, using signals such
as audience segment, context, and price floor. The highest eligible bid wins
and its creative is served, usually within a few hundred milliseconds.

A private marketplace (PMP) is an invitation-only version of this auction, where
a publisher offers selected inventory to chosen buyers through a deal. Deals let
buyers reach premium inventory with more control than the fully open exchange,
at the cost of scale.
