# Buyer personas

A buyer persona is a semi-fictional profile of an ideal customer, built from
real research rather than guesswork. A useful persona captures goals, the job
the person is trying to get done, the obstacles in their way, and where they go
for information.

Good personas come from interviews and behavioural data, not from a brainstorm.
Five honest interviews beat fifty invented traits. Watch for the difference
between what people say they want and what their actions reveal.

Personas are a tool for focus. If a message would resonate with the persona it
stays; if it would not, it goes. Teams that keep personas vague end up writing
for everyone and connecting with no one.
