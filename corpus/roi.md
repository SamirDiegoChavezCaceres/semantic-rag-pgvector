# Return on Investment (ROI) in marketing

ROI measures how much profit a campaign returns for every unit of money spent.
The basic formula is:

    ROI = (revenue attributed to marketing - marketing cost) / marketing cost

A ROI of 2.0 (or 200%) means every dollar spent returned two dollars of profit
on top of the original dollar. Because the denominator is cost, ROI rewards
both raising revenue and lowering spend.

A common mistake is to attribute all revenue to the last touch. Multi-touch
attribution spreads credit across the touchpoints a buyer actually saw, which
usually lowers the headline ROI of any single channel but gives a more honest
picture of what is working.
